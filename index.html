HOW TO PLAY

Herd Mentality Rules

1

Join the Herd

Your goal is to guess what the MAJORITY of the room will pick. If your answer is in the majority, you score 1 point.

2

The Lone Wolf Stigma

If you are the ONLY person in the room to pick an option, you receive the Lone Wolf Stigma 🐺.

3

Passing the Stigma

The Stigma stays with you until someone else becomes the new Lone Wolf. If you hold the Stigma at the end of the game, you cannot win.

4

Winning the Game

There is no time limit for voting. The game ends immediately when a player reaches 10 points.
